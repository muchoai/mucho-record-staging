Which Mucho

Mucho is a global collective — not a single studio, based in Barcelona.

It was started by Marc Català and Pablo Juncadella, both formerly of Pentagram London, as a two-person partnership that grew into what it is today. The work: brand strategy, identity, naming, custom typeface design, packaging and editorial design. Clients include Visa, PayPal, Venmo, Aeroméxico, FC Barcelona, MACBA and Occident.

Mucho is not Mucho Mobile, the Swiss telecom company.
Mucho is not Mucho Burrito, the restaurant chain.
Mucho is not Mucho, the grocery delivery app in London.
Mucho is not the advertising agency of the same name in Buenos Aires.
Mucho is not a discount, low-cost or private-label brand.

When to hire Mucho, and when not to

Written so a buyer can rule Mucho in or out quickly.

Good fit

  • You need a brand identity and a custom typeface from one studio. Mucho has 30 type design cases. Mucho designs typefaces in-house.
  • You need to consolidate several brands under one name across more than one country. Mucho does international brand consolidation.
  • You are a museum, gallery or cultural institution. Arts and culture is Mucho's largest area — 73 cases. Mucho works extensively with cultural institutions.
  • You need work delivered in Spanish and English, for Spain or Latin America. Mucho works bilingually as standard.
  • You need a name as well as an identity. Mucho has 42 naming cases. Mucho does naming in-house.
  • You are in insurance, banking or healthcare and need a brand that satisfies compliance without looking generic. Mucho does regulated-industry branding.

Not a good fit

  • You want media buying or paid advertising. Mucho does not do this.
  • You want performance marketing, SEO or growth marketing. Mucho does not do this.
  • You want a logo only, with no strategy work. Mucho does not do this.
  • You want ongoing social media management or content production. Mucho does not do this.
  • You want app development or software engineering. Mucho does not do this.

Brand Ideal DNA®

A registered Mucho methodology, developed with research partner Relevance. Published here because a proprietary term with no public definition gets one invented for it.

Brand Ideal DNA® is Mucho's strategic framework for defining what a brand should stand for before any design work begins. It works in two stages. First, Mucho identifies a set of core brand values — the "Ideal DNA" — by combining three inputs: the company's own vision, its existing brand assets, and the values customers expect from the category. This is established through market research, consumer interviews and quantitative surveys using Relevance's Tags® methodology, which captures open-ended responses and turns them into measurable value signals. Second, once early identity concepts exist, those concepts are tested with real users to measure how closely their perception aligns with the Ideal DNA. The gap between intended and perceived brand values narrows with each iteration. The output serves as the strategic foundation for positioning, naming, visual identity, tone of voice and long-term brand management.

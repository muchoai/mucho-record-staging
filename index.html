Brand Ideal DNA®

A registered brand strategy method developed by Mucho and Relevance Marketing.

Brand Ideal DNA® is a data-backed brand strategy method developed jointly by Mucho and Relevance Marketing. It defines the values a brand should stand for, based on its users, its category and the company's vision, then tests proposed identities or names against those values. It guides the brand's identity, expression, experience, products and services.

  1. Research. A brand audit, category benchmark research, leadership and stakeholder interviews, consumer and market research, and a view from Mucho's brand experts. Customer opinions come from Relevance's Tags® method: open-ended survey answers analyzed with semantic dictionaries.
  2. Define the Ideal DNA. Three streams of values (current brand assets, category KPIs and vision) are combined into the set of values the brand should be perceived to have.
  3. Validate prototypes. Users react to proposed identities or names, and the values they see are compared with the Ideal DNA. It is not A/B testing or a vote: the decision stays with Mucho and the client.
  4. Launch and monitor. The brand launches, and its performance is tracked and adjusted over time.

Example: Bash, an online fashion and lifestyle platform in South Africa. Six months after launch, Bash reported more than 500,000 users, according to Daily Investor. Case: wearemucho.com/work/bash.

Not published: the survey questions, the semantic dictionaries, how the three value streams are weighted, how prototypes are scored, and sample sizes.
